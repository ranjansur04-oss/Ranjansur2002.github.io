About me

Clear reasoning, supported by technology.

I am a Mathematics postgraduate from Jajpur, Odisha. I completed my M.Sc. at Sambalpur University with First Class (7.93/10 CGPA). My GATE 2026 rank of 1789 and Odisha CPET 2023 state rank of 148 show command of advanced concepts under competitive conditions.

In teaching, I want mathematics to feel logical. I start with the idea behind a formula, then present each solution as short, ordered steps that a student can follow and repeat.

Programming supports that approach. I build learning material in LaTeX and apply Python, R and MATLAB to support data-driven teaching. AI-assisted tools help me draft and refine content, and Google Classroom, Zoom and Microsoft Teams carry it to students online and in hybrid settings.

  • FocusHigher Secondary (+2 Science) and undergraduate mathematics
  • MethodInteractive problem-solving and logical reasoning ahead of memorization
  • DeliveryDigital and hybrid teaching with Bloom's Taxonomy-aligned lessons
